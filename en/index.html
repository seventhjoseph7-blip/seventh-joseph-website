How it works

FAQ

What people ask before starting

Is my financial data safe?

The automation runs in systems and accounts that belong to you, and our access is limited to what we need to build it. We are ready to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before we see your data.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

Every automation has a controller who checks the results, either someone from your team (trained free for 3 months) or one of ours. Uncertain items are flagged for a person to review.

Our software is unusual. Can you still work with it?

That is what the free analysis is for. We check your software and workflow first, then tell you what can be automated before any cost.

What if only part of the work can be automated?

The fee follows the percentage. Example: if the automation takes over 60% of the work of a staff member earning IDR 5 million, the build fee is 2 × 60% × IDR 5 million = IDR 6 million.

When do I pay?

After the automation passes the written acceptance criteria we agree on before the build starts.

What if our software changes or updates?

Your controller will notice first. For adjusting the automation, a maintenance service is available, priced as needed.

Do you work with foreign-owned companies (PT PMA)?

Yes. We work in English and Russian, and set up admin and finance systems for companies operating in Indonesia, including in Bali.
